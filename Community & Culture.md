# Learning Day Community & Culture

## The Learning Day T-Shirt Challenge
- **Requirement:** Attend 4 sessions within a 6-month window to earn an official Learning Day T-shirt.
- **Feedback Form:** Shown at the conclusion of every Learning Day: [NLB Form](http://bit.ly/nlbform)

---

## Origins & History
- **Online Sessions Launch:** Friday, 3 April 2020 (**Learning Day 67**), created to keep the community connected and curious during the COVID-19 lockdown circuit breaker.
- **Cadence:** The community increased frequency from bi-weekly to weekly during lockdown periods, introducing midway ".5" sessions (e.g., LD 68.5, LD 110.5, LD 111.5).
- **Etherpads & Zoom:** Sessions routinely used shared riseup pads (e.g. `pad.riseup.net/p/ld...`) and Zoom rooms for collaborative real-time note-taking and feedback.

### Future Learning Day Ideas
1. **Magic School Bus Learning Day:** Chartering a friend's private bus and conducting lightning talks while driving around Singapore.
2. **Walking Learning Day:** Walking around a Singapore neighborhood pointing out historical, architectural, or ecological curiosities (with scheduled food & snack pitstops), plus a Johor Bahru (JB) edition.
3. **Cooking Learning Day:** Hosted in a kitchen venue combining cooking and learning.
4. **Cafe-Hopping Learning Day:** Meetups rotating across cozy cafes.
5. **Anti-Learning Day (April Fools' Edition):** PowerPoint karaoke with humorous, completely fabricated topics.
6. **Mime / Non-Verbal Learning Day:** Presenting without spoken words.
7. **Keyword Penalty Learning Day:** Presenters or participants who utter chosen banned words face playful penalties.
8. **Hands-On Game & Media Days:** Sessions dedicated to tabletop card games, video game mechanics, or book/movie analysis.
