### 8. [[WP]]  — AMA - Was on a human library (NACLI). Coaching, training for 15 years as an introvert. Used to do ClashOfClans competitively. Geopolitics and investment, used to work in banking. Transitioned from executive/career coaching into life coaching, for broader impact. https://coachingfederation.org/. Health issues led to more introspection and seeking coaching for what's worth doing with life. Mental, physical, dietary changes and improvement alleviated and mitigated health issues dramatically. Coping with rejection from youth and old, finding places that can fit in, building confidence around self. Coaching certification is relevant and important in Singapore to be recognised (but schools offer different quality of training for certification). Best practice: Ask coaches to tell stories of who they worked with, how they helped, NOT testimonials. Identify authenticity and real-ness.
