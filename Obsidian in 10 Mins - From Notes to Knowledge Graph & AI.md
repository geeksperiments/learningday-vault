## 1. The Dilemma: OneNote vs. The Plain Text Epiphany (2 mins)

### The Relatable Story
- *"For day-to-day manual notes, I still use MS OneNote."*
- OneNote is great for freeform typing, but traditional note-taking apps have a fundamental limitation: **they are walled gardens**.
  - Your notes are locked in a proprietary database or cloud service.
  - If the service changes pricing, goes down, or you want to export your data, you are stuck.

### The Obsidian Difference: Local-First Plain Text
- In Obsidian, there is **no proprietary database**. Every single note is just a plain `.md` (Markdown) file saved in a regular folder on your laptop.
- **Why this matters to everyone**:
  1. **Future-Proof**: 20 years from now, plain text files will still be readable on any device.
  2. **100% Offline & Private**: Your thoughts belong to you, not a cloud server.
  3. **Zero Cost / No Subscription**: Core Obsidian is free forever.

## 2. Linked Thinking: The Concept Behind the Magic (3 mins)

### The "Folder Trap" (Hierarchical Paralysis)
- In traditional apps, every note must live in a folder:
  `Projects > 2026 > Meetups > Learning Day > Talks`
- **The problem**: What happens when a talk is about both *AI* and *Psychology*? Which folder does it belong to? You end up with taxonomy fatigue.

### The Wikipedia Approach (Zettelkasten Made Simple)
- Don't worry about complex Zettelkasten jargon (forget "slip-boxes" or numbering systems).
- **The core idea is simple: Think like Wikipedia.**
  - Wikipedia doesn't put articles into rigid folders—it connects them with **links**.
  - In Obsidian, you type `[[` and link any word to another note: e.g., `[[Aaron]]` or `[[Artificial Intelligence]]`.

### The Magic: Automatic Backlinks
- When you link to a note, Obsidian creates a **two-way connection**.
- *Example*: If you create a note for a speaker `[[Darren]]` in your talk notes, whenever you open Darren's note, Obsidian's **Backlinks panel** automatically shows every single session Darren has ever spoken at—**without you ever updating a master list**.

## 4. The Modern Superpower: Markdown + AI (1.5 mins)

### Why Plain Text Beats Cloud Apps in the Age of AI
- Because Obsidian notes are standard Markdown files on disk, **local AI tools, scripts, and agents can directly interact with them**.
- **Real-world example**:
  - While attending a tech conference or meetup, you can snap photos of presentation slides or drop quick voice memos.
  - An AI agent can read those slides, extract the core ideas, format them cleanly into Markdown, and file them into your Obsidian vault with proper `[[links]]` and citations in real time.
- Traditional apps like OneNote or Apple Notes make this painful behind closed APIs; Obsidian makes it effortless because it's just files on disk.

## 5. Golden Rule for Beginners: How to Start (1 min)

### The #1 Beginner Mistake
- Spending 3 days watching YouTube tutorials, downloading 40 community plugins, and configuring complex themes **before writing a single word**.

### The 3-Step Starter Formula:
1. **Start Vanilla**: Download Obsidian, open an empty folder, and just write.
2. **Link When You Notice Repetition**: When you see a person, project, or concept appear twice, surround it with `[[double brackets]]`.
3. **Let Structure Emerge**: Don't force a structure on Day 1. Let your knowledge graph grow organically over time.
